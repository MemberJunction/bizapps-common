Add `DashboardFilterBarComponent` (`bizapps-dashboard-filter-bar`), a period and company filter bar for BizApps dashboards, with the pure period-window, period-state and ID-list helpers it applies. The period dropdown offers this, last and next quarter, this and last year, all time and a custom range; quarters and years follow a fiscal year start the app supplies through `ResolveFiscalYearStart`. App-specific filters are projected into the bar.
